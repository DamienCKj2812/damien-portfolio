## Entrance and verification

- The integration builder carves the street aperture in `tower_portal_clearance` front skin and clears the interior atrium cavity from both front and `tower_atrium_clearance` rear skins. Keep the exterior front band; the front cylinder also wraps around the lobby sides and otherwise blocks balcony/tree sightlines. The builder hides the obsolete flat podium and recesses the working door/pocket assemblies by 1.8 m in memory. Preserve the slide timing and lobby handoff.
- Generated `lobbyPortal` metadata describes the street-facing aperture. Runtime clips preloaded lobby geometry/reflections to its four camera-dependent planes while outside, then releases clipping at the threshold; this prevents lobby walls covering the outdoor skyline when the package reveals. Keep the shared basis conversion, reverse navigation and fixed plane count. Check `node --import tsx scripts/verify_lobby_portal.mts`.
- Seal the recessed entrance with continuous side/header/floor returns and overlapping closed leaves. Fit the fixed pocket casings to the fully-open leaf envelope and enclose their side/back/top/bottom faces. Oversized flat pockets can obscure the neighboring curved storefront; uncovered recess depth can reveal the lobby through closed-door gaps.
