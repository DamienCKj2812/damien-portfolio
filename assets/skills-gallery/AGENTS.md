# Skills-gallery guidance

- Approved Skills content comes from the completed About data (`src/data/portfolio.json`) and Projects catalogue (`assets/project-hallway/projects.json` / `docs/project-catalogue.md`). Run `node --import tsx scripts/build_skills_catalogue.mts` to generate `skills.json`; it reads JSON directly and hashes canonical JSON bytes for provenance, distinguishing project evidence, profile-only tools and ongoing learning rather than inventing proficiency ratings.
- For content-only changes use `blender --background assets/skills-gallery/skills-gallery.blend --python-exit-code 1 --python assets/skills-gallery/refresh_skill_content.py`. Its fingerprint guards architecture, sculpture, visitor transforms/geometry/keys and the guided rig, and retains a recovery master. Do not rebuild the whole scene merely to update text.
- Nine skill cards share Projects' exact `ProjectCardFocusControls` v2 shell through `variant="skill"`, with eight skill-specific sections. Native picks, centered/hover prompts and index selection enter `view="skill"`; preserve the route/look and original tour pause state on Back/Escape. Focus hides visitors and the centre sculpture to keep every card readable, then restores both; AT-AT inspection retains its existing workflow. Check `node --import tsx scripts/verify_skills_content.mts` and, after build, `node --import tsx scripts/verify_skills_focus.mts`.
- Mobile card detail uses the shared chapter header/progress bar, readable section panel, fixed Previous/Next dock and native chapter picker over translucent live-room backgrounds. Keep Skills-specific labels and evidence sections; Escape closes the chapter picker before dismissing detail. After build, also run `DETAIL_TEST_LEVEL=2 node --import tsx scripts/verify_project_detail_mobile.mts` for responsive bounds, section selection and saved gallery return.

- Master `skills-gallery.blend`, scene `SKILLS / Technology Gallery`; one `.blend1` recovery file. Full build: `blender --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python assets/skills-gallery/build_gallery.py` (add `-- --render`).
- `gallery_walk.py` owns the 2940-frame/98-second guided route. Only the player rig's location is keyed; its camera is unkeyed and has no auto-aim constraints. Exhibit jumps must preserve look.
- Six visitor roots contain 90 articulated point meshes. Four move in reserved lanes; root/part Cycles modifiers repeat the native clip with frame 960 duplicating frame 1. Export 959 unique frames independently of the 2940-frame player route.
- Browser visitor time is independent of walking (`animationFollowsWalk=false`). Keep full cross-time player/NPC clearance checks in `validate_guided_walk`; phase-offset-only checks are insufficient when the player stops or jumps. `update_autonomous_visitors.py` is the focused master-saving NPC update.
- `atat_walker.py` owns the reference-led centre exhibit: black occluding armor and fine white native contour curves, four mechanical legs, a ringed neck and armored head. Its root has `centre_exhibit_id='atat'`; preserve mesh picking and the `atat` close-up camera. The T-Rex is retired.
- `replace_trex_with_atat.py` is the explicit master-saving sculpture update. It verifies other object/visitor/camera transforms and keeps one `.blend1` backup. Dinosaur conversion scripts are historical; do not replay them on the AT-AT gallery.
- `Gallery Walk Preview.py` is embedded but opt-in. `test_guided_walk.py` checks look/input math and synthetic modal events in background mode, not native GUI event playback.
- Export: `blender --background assets/skills-gallery/skills-gallery.blend --python-exit-code 1 --python assets/journey/export_browser_room.py -- --level skills`. Then package/source checks in [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md).
- Export the closest animated part/root world transforms; dropping child keys loses real gait. Native Skills channels are stride 12; XYZ guided positions are a separate `route.bin`. Read metadata, not hallway stride assumptions.
