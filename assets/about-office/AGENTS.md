# About-office guidance

- Master: `about-office.blend`, scene `ABOUT / Skyline Office`. Keep one `.blend1` recovery file; do not save a separate static/contact version.
- Full build: `blender --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python assets/about-office/build_office.py`. Add `-- --render` for previews; generation replaces manual edits.
- The card is a flat 90 × 55 × 0.8 mm paper mesh under `Office • Name card / interactive assembly`; its top `contact face` has `CardUV`. Keep the authored contact camera, but no automatic Blender camera zoom. Idle shine animates independently.
- `Office • Observer / looking out at city` is a point-source mesh. Read-only export derives its invisible `observerPick`; React opens `ObserverProfile` without changing the camera/card view. Content lives in canonical `src/data/portfolio.json`, exposed to React by the validated `src/data/portfolio.ts` wrapper, not Blender or the exporter.
- The Observer profile uses the redesigned Identity / Portrait / Record overlay: desktop independent columns, narrow-screen section navigation and expandable experience highlights, fixed header/office-return footer, stack tags and contact Copy controls. Keep the existing `PixelPortrait` SVG-masked original-color hover trail, keyboard full-photo reveal and reduced-motion/cleanup behavior inside the framed portrait. Profile redesigns are React/CSS changes and do not require rebuilding the office or portrait assets. Preserve modal input blocking, Escape and opener-focus/camera restoration. After build, run `node --import tsx scripts/verify_observer_profile_browser.mts`; runtime details are in [city guidance](../../src/components/city/AGENTS.md).
- About display target: `Office • About content screen / web texture target`, with `ScreenUV`. Placeholder geometry/labels can be replaced without merging the screen into the monitor body.
- `malaysia_screen.py` owns the black/white Malaysia map parented to that display. It caches public-domain Natural Earth 1:50m country geography, keeps Peninsula and Sabah/Sarawak at one scale, and hides the original screen network/navigation/globe/chart for recovery. Focused update: `update_malaysia_screen.py`; the main builder reapplies the same map. Preserve screen UVs, workstation/card/Observer, cameras and aquarium actions. Export tags `officeScreenMap` groups and `officeScreen` provenance; check `verify_malaysia_screen.py` after exporting About.
- Website routing uses level `about`, entrance `(0,-6.8,0)`, authored presentation/entry/contact cameras. Don't substitute the review free-look camera or rotate the native master to fit the cabin.
- Export: `blender --background assets/about-office/about-office.blend --python-exit-code 1 --python assets/journey/export_browser_room.py -- --level about`; then `node --import tsx assets/journey/verify_rooms.mts`.
- Old nameplate/zoom patches are construction history. Current generator includes the flat card/idle hint; normal updates belong in the main file.
- Card lettering reads name/email/phone/GitHub/WhatsApp directly from `src/data/portfolio.json` through `contact_card_content.py`, without regex extraction of app source. Run `update_contact_card.py` against the master for copy-only changes, then export About. It fits seven printed lines and verifies surrounding transforms/actions; do not rebuild the entire office for contact text changes.
- The read-only About exporter derives invisible `contactLinkPick` faces from the GitHub/WhatsApp font bounds. React activates them only in card focus; preserve their alignment, drag rejection and separate broad opener/outside-dismissal behavior.
- `office_environment.py`, called by the generator, owns the right-wall art panel/low console/end bookshelf, left-wall inset aquarium, and plain rear wall. Preserve its local RNG and the 2.6 × 3.2 m entry passage. Rear panels carry `office_entry_door` metadata and export as `officeDoor`; the browser hides these authored panels and uses the shared clickable `RoomElevatorEntrance` instead. Three fish and 16 bubble roots have native cyclic animation over 720 frames; `Aquarium loop frames` and `Aquarium animation bounds` supply export/playback metadata. Preserve seamless turns and bubble fade/recycle boundaries. Static plants/rocks stay outside these moving roots.
