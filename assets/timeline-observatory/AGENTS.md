# Observatory guidance

- Master `timeline-observatory.blend`; `node --import tsx assets/timeline-observatory/build_timeline_content.mts` generates `milestones.json` directly from confirmed `src/data/portfolio.json` records and the Level 03 project catalogue, without VM evaluation of app source. Keep the room NPC-free; the builder imports no characters or other masters.
- For content-only updates: run the Node content generator, then `update_timeline_content.py` against the master and re-export Experience. The focused updater preserves room geometry/transforms, six celestial animations and the walking route. Do not perform a full room rebuild for copy changes.
- `prepare_timeline_logos.py` owns original-color PNG copies of the supplied APU/LYJ/COS artwork; originals stay untouched in `assets/media/logos/organisations/`, registered by `assets/media/manifest.json`. Keep their requested regular colors, without a grayscale filter or browser tint. Run preparation before content updates/full builds. `timeline_board_art.py` packs separate UV logo quads, hides replaced procedural symbols and fits qualification headings/specialism text. Education display labels belong to the `cardTitle`/`cardHeading`/`cardSpecialism` fields in `src/data/portfolio.json`, preserving confirmed qualification records. The exporter publishes logo PNGs with all buffers/hash; verify `verify_board_art.py` after export.
- Six real entries cover the APU diploma, LYJ, COS Great Trading, freelance MyRumawip/DWMLight/Amplifii work, APU bachelor's degree (final results CGPA 3.36, expected June 2027), and current focus. Focused cards share the Level 03 case-file component/layout, scroll/keyboard section navigation and responsive card framing; Back/Escape restores the saved route/look.
- Build: `blender --background --python-exit-code 1 --python assets/timeline-observatory/build_timeline_observatory.py -- --no-render`. It resets factory data and saves compressed with `save_version=0`; preserve manual edits/recovery before a full rebuild.
- `-- --preview-only --all-previews` renders the existing master. Native geometry/route checks are `validate_timeline_observatory.py` and `verify_observatory_walk.py` in background Blender.
- Only the movement rig's location is keyed on the 2400-frame/80-second route. Camera/rig look is unkeyed and unconstrained; station jumps and wrapping must preserve yaw/pitch.
- The globe/planet clock is independent of walking pause. Browser Pause animation/reduced motion/offscreen/unmount freezes it; there are six animated celestial channels and no NPC channels.
- Four observation windows are actual curved openings showing five exterior planets/starfield, not flat banner art. Walls use opaque satin-black panels and eight shallow architectural ribs with recessed white LEDs; keep the brighter lower lights and roof oculus clear. Ceiling is smooth with architectural light rings, not a wireframe grid.
- Export uses the semantic level `experience`, not `observatory`: `blender --background assets/timeline-observatory/timeline-observatory.blend --python-exit-code 1 --python assets/journey/export_browser_room.py -- --level experience`.
- Source comparison: `blender --background assets/timeline-observatory/timeline-observatory.blend --python-exit-code 1 --python assets/journey/verify_room_source.py -- --level experience`; also run `node --import tsx assets/journey/verify_rooms.mts`.
- Focus regression: `node --import tsx scripts/verify_timeline_focus.mts` checks actual content and every card's responsive bounds; also run the existing project-card focus check after shared focus/UI changes.
