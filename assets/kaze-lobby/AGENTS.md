# Lobby model guidance

- `kaze-lobby-walkthrough.blend` is the sole master: architecture, static cameras, navigation and R1 door animation are in the same file. `.blend1` is recovery, not another model.
- Full rebuild: `blender --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python assets/kaze-lobby/rebuild_lobby.py`. Add `-- --render` for previews or `-- --output-dir /tmp/opencode/lobby-check` for an isolated rebuild.
- Visible branding is KAJU. `../branding/kaju_brand.py` owns the shared triangular mark and spaced wordmark; the pillar and directory use it. Focused updates use `../branding/apply_kaju_branding.py -- --model lobby`. Existing scene/object IDs are retained for integration lookups.
- `build_lobby.py` and `add_navigation.py` are internal stages; the rebuild defers saving until both complete. Historical pillar/stair/crowd patches can replace animated objects and should not be replayed on the finished master.
- Navigation is 1–1020 at 30 FPS: six-second right-escalator ride, stop outside R1 at 840, doors open 900–990, handoff at 1020. Change source timing and both navigation JSON exports together.
- R1 leaves and their outline curves must move together; seam visibility and black interior backing are intentional in the authored file. Aperture fitting/backing removal belongs to the read-only journey export copy.
- L1/L2/R1/R2 landing status typography shares Z=9.40 above the portal. `align_elevator_displays.py` is a focused master-saving correction with camera/door motion checks; keep the same baseline in full generation/navigation.
- Entrance `(0,-12,0)` aligns by `(-1.5,6.45,0)` in the integration copy. Do not translate only its floor/doorway to fit the city.
- Browser export: `blender --background assets/kaze-lobby/kaze-lobby-walkthrough.blend --python-exit-code 1 --python assets/journey/export_browser_lobby.py`; connected follow-up/verification is in [journey workflow](../journey/WORKFLOW.md).
- Video banners and complete startup preloading are [planned](../../docs/roadmap.md), not current Blender or browser behavior.
- `fish_motion.py` installs the native hovering/swimming rig during a full rebuild. Body/head sway, nested tail swish and individual fin flutter share the 1019-frame independent lobby loop. Keep point membranes, fin rays and outlines under the same part rigs; the exporter must use the closest animated ancestor. The projector, halos and architecture remain static. Check with `verify_fish_motion.py` and its browser-package companion `verify_fish_motion.mjs`.
- `npc_motion.py` owns four editable walking shuttles and four native morph channels per person, with staggered role-specific conversation, wave, typing, phone and looking gestures for all 17 people. Escalators have no rider NPC. Accessories follow hand motion through their own autonomous export channels. `add_npc_motion.py` is a focused master-saving installation that checks navigation/door transforms; full rebuild also installs it. `verify_npc_motion.py` checks gestures, planted feet and loop seams. Frame 1020 duplicates NPC frame 1 (1019 unique frames). Browser NPC time is independent; camera and R1 doors remain journey-driven. Lobby exports use stride 16 for native morph weights.
